Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Bewlet is a personal-finance web application designed to keep each user in control of their financial information.

Information Bewlet processes

  • Your Google email address, name, profile image, registration status, pricing region, and license status are used to authenticate and manage access.
  • Payment screenshots are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and are available only to the registering user and authorized Bewlet administrators.
  • Feedback and issue reports, including attachments you choose to submit, are sent to Bewlet's support storage.

Your financial data

Transactions, wallets, categories, budgets, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device for offline use. After you authorize Google Drive, Bewlet creates and synchronizes a dedicated spreadsheet and backup files in your own Google Drive.

Google access

Bewlet requests the limited drive.file permission. This allows Bewlet to create and manage files it creates for you; it does not grant Bewlet general access to every file in your Google Drive. Google refresh credentials are encrypted before storage.

Sharing and advertising

Bewlet does not sell financial information or use transaction data for advertising. Service providers such as Google, Supabase, Vercel, and the configured feedback service process limited data only to operate the application.

Retention and control

You may export your data, disconnect Google Drive, delete local records, or request account deletion from Bewlet Settings. You may choose whether Drive files are retained when deleting your Bewlet account.

Security

Bewlet uses authenticated sessions, private storage, row-level access controls, and encrypted Google refresh credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their Google account and devices.
