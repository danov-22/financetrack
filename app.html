Privacy, ownership & offline use

You own your financial data. Bewlet does not sell transaction information or use it for advertising.

  • On this device: changes are saved locally so the app can continue working offline.
  • When connected: pending changes are synchronized after internet access returns.
  • Your Google Sheet: once account sync is released, each user’s finance records will be stored in their own Google Drive—not the owner’s finance sheet.
  • Bewlet access: only feedback and issue reports are sent to Bewlet’s support storage.

Closing or refreshing while offline will not intentionally discard saved local changes. Do not clear browser storage before synchronization is complete.
